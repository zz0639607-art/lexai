    FAQ+
    Questions

    Frequently asked

    Everything you need to know about LexAI.

    Is LexAI a real law firm? ▼
    No. LexAI is an AI-powered informational tool — not a law firm, not a legal services provider, and not a substitute for a licensed attorney. We do not provide legal advice or legal representation of any kind. No attorney-client relationship is formed. For any important legal matter, always consult a qualified attorney licensed in your jurisdiction.
    Is my contract data private? ▼
    Contract text passes through the LexAI backend to the configured AI provider. Account, session and usage records are stored. Text and reports you explicitly save to Contract History are also stored in your account database and backups; you can delete them from history. Provider retention policies also apply. Remove unnecessary personal information before submitting.
    Which languages are supported? ▼
    LexAI can analyze contracts in over 200 languages, including Chinese, Arabic, Spanish, French, Japanese, Korean, German, Hindi, Portuguese, Russian, and many more. You can also request your report in any of these languages.
    How accurate is the AI analysis? ▼
    LexAI uses Google Gemini, one of the world's most advanced language models. It is highly effective at identifying common risk patterns, unusual clauses, and missing protections. However, no AI is perfect — results should be a starting point, not a final legal opinion.
    Do I need my own AI API key? ▼
    No. The operator configures the AI provider on the server. Never enter an API key into this page.
    What types of contracts can LexAI analyze? ▼
    LexAI can analyze virtually any written contract: employment agreements, rental leases, NDAs, service agreements, purchase contracts, franchise agreements, loan agreements, partnership agreements, IP licensing deals, and more.
